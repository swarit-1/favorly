# Mockup generation prompts

All four images were generated with the built-in image generation tool using the `ui-mockup` use case. They are high-fidelity directional concepts for a Flutter/Material 3 mobile app.

## Shared visual brief

Create a realistic, shippable mobile product UI for Favorly, a neighbor-to-neighbor errand coordination app. Favorly is mutual-aid infrastructure, not a delivery marketplace. Use warm off-white `#FAF7F0`, deep evergreen `#214E3B`, soft apricot `#F2A56B`, charcoal text, pale sage success fills, and pale amber review states. Use clean friendly geometric sans-serif typography, generous touch targets, crisp hierarchy, and practical Material 3 patterns. Avoid gig-worker dashboards, ecommerce styling, maps, competitive gamification, technical AI jargon, purple palettes, glassmorphism, fantasy UI, trademarks, and watermarks.

## 01 — Trip feed and posting

Design a landscape board with exactly three tall phone screens: the Maple St · Building B trip feed, a `Post a trip` form with store/time/requester/dollar/item caps and optional voice input, and a `Here’s what we heard` confirmation screen populated from the transcript “I’m going to Trader Joe’s at three, max five people, forty bucks each.” Make the active Trader Joe’s trip, remaining spots, `Add my list`, and `Post a trip` actions immediately scannable.

## 02 — Request intake and review

Design a landscape board with exactly three tall phone screens: `Add your list` with equal `Type it`, `Say it`, and `Snap a list` choices; `Check your list` with Bananas, Oat milk, Sourdough, Unsalted butter, and a pale-amber `Lemons · 2` row labeled `Check this`; and a success state `Your list is with Ana` with a simple trip status timeline. Make AI uncertainty specific and editable without showing technical confidence scores.

## 03 — Shopping and substitution

Design a landscape board with exactly three tall phone screens: Ana’s aisle-ordered merged shopping list with requester chips and caps; a `Find a substitute` shelf camera for Chloe’s unsalted butter with a $6 cap; and Chloe’s realtime `Choose a substitute` screen with a countdown, three candidate cards, prices, reasons, a skip action, and `Choose this`. Preserve requester identity and budget context through the entire handoff.

## 04 — Receipt, settlement, and ledger

Design a landscape board with exactly three tall phone screens: `Check the receipt` showing reconciled totals and only one ambiguous `EURO BUTTER · $4.49` assignment; `You owe Ana $18.42` with itemized lines, proportional tax, external Venmo payment, and `Favorly never handles your money`; and the Maple St · Building B reciprocity ledger after delivery with trips run, favors received, carried value, and the explanation `A simple history of giving and receiving — no scores, no rankings.`
